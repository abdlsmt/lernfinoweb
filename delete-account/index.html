Delete account

Delete learning data on this device

If you use Lernfino without an account, open Settings and choose Reset Learning Data. You can also uninstall the app to remove its local data from the device.

Delete a Lernfino account

In the app, open Settings, choose My account, then Delete account and confirm. This removes the account and synced data, including authentication details, learning progress, saved vocabulary, scores, transcripts and AI feedback. In-app deletion also clears account progress and queued drafts on that phone. Anonymous cost metrics and a random deletion receipt are retained.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, prepare a deletion request and send it from the email address associated with your Lernfino account. Requests are processed and verified within seven business days. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
