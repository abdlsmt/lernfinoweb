What you’ll learn

Build all four German skills, then put them to work in exam practice and everyday situations.

Learning path

Begin with Starter S1–S10, then follow the path through A1 to B2 with short lessons you can fit into your day.

Full mock exams

Take eight independent full mock exams in Goethe and ÖSD formats. Practice Hören and Lesen separately, with telc-format exercise sets also available.

Feedback on Sprechen and Schreiben

Get optional AI feedback on spoken and written answers. Evaluation requires consent and is subject to account and plan limits.

Life in Germany

Practice 28 everyday scenarios, from finding an apartment to visiting a doctor. Read the dialogues, do exercises and take a speaking turn in role-play.

Stories and vocabulary

Read and listen to leveled stories. Look up words in context, then save them to My words.

Review and progress

Use the mistake notebook to revisit errors and spaced repetition to practice words. Switch to dark mode whenever you like.

How Lernfino works

  1. Find your level

    Start from scratch or take the web placement test to see where to begin.

  2. Take a short lesson

    Follow the learning path and practice a little at a time.

  3. Use what you learned

    Try a real-life dialogue, review words or work through a timed mock exam.
