CONTEXTUAL DICTIONARY & GRAMMAR
Preposition

Brief

letter
Grammar & Context Note:

Two-way preposition. Answers 'Where?' and therefore takes the Dative case: 'hinter ihrem Haus'.

EXAMPLE SENTENCE

Ich schreibe meiner Mutter einen langen Brief.

The garden is behind the building.

WHAT'S INSIDE LERNFINO?

A Complete Ecosystem for Learning German

Graded reading stories, interactive video lessons, a contextual dictionary, and Goethe/ÖSD exam-format practice united in one app.

A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 GRADED LIBRARY

Leveled Audio Stories & Books

Read leveled A1–B2 stories and articles with German narration.

  • Word-by-word synchronized audio highlighting and playback speed control
  • “Listen-First” mode to train your ear before revealing the text
  • “Roleplay Character” mode to take speaking turns in story dialogues
  • Sentence translations and end-of-chapter comprehension quizzes
  • Start from zero: a step-by-step A1–B2 learning path with short lessons
VIDEO LESSONS & SHORTS

Interactive Video & Shorts

Watch the interactive episode Brief im falschen Kasten (Tim & Lea, A2) and vertical Shorts clips, or switch to “Study as Lesson” mode with scene-by-scene checkpoints.

  • Interactive comprehension questions pausing automatically at scene ends
  • Tap any word in the video transcript to open its dictionary card
  • Vertical Shorts lessons with quick mini-quizzes
  • Life in Germany: everyday scenarios with dialogues, exercises, and role-play
SMART DICTIONARY & SRS

Contextual Vocabulary Notebook

Noun articles (der/die/das), plurals, and verb conjugations at your fingertips. Every saved word preserves the story and video sentences where you met it.

AUDIO DICTIONARY

Lernfino's vocabulary archive includes word audio, article guides, plurals, and real story examples from A1 to B2.

  • Spaced repetition algorithm (SRS) for structured daily review
  • View saved story contexts directly on your flashcard
  • Clear, learner-friendly grammar notes
  • Grammar trainers: articles, cases & prepositions, sentence building, and verbs
  • 15 interface languages: use the app in your own language
GOETHE & ÖSD FORMAT PRACTICE

Goethe/ÖSD Exam-Format Practice

Timed Goethe/ÖSD format Hören and Lesen practice exams across A1–B2, exercise sets, writing tasks, and speaking sets—plus optional AI feedback (explicit consent for adults 18 and over required).

  • Hören & Lesen Practice Exams: Timed Hören and Lesen exams across A1–B2 with step navigation and audio evidence jumps
  • Schreiben & Sprechen Tasks: Schreiben writing tasks and Sprechen speaking sets with model texts and optional AI evaluation for adults 18 and over (with explicit consent, subject to plan limits)
  • Exam Exercise Sets: A1–B2 sets covering Grammatik, Hören, Lesen, and Wortschatz
  • Exam vs. Study Modes: Practice with format timers or study freely at your own settings
  • 8 full practice exams & telc-format sets: eight complete Goethe/ÖSD-format mock exams plus independent telc-format exercise sets
