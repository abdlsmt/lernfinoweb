Can I study offline?

Lernfino stores vocabulary, story progress and practice results on your device. Download the available lesson media while connected to use it offline. The available content depends on your plan. AI evaluation needs internet access.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

On the old device, open Settings, choose Backup Learning Data and transfer the resulting JSON file to your new device. In Settings on the new device, choose the backup file to restore your progress.

How does exam practice work?

The eight full mock exams are independent study material in Goethe and ÖSD formats. Take a complete timed exam or practice Lesen, Hören, Schreiben and Sprechen separately. The optional AI evaluations for Schreiben and Sprechen require explicit consent from an adult aged 18 or over.

How do I use the story and dictionary audio?

Play a story to follow along with its highlighted text. You can adjust the playback speed or tap a word for its contextual dictionary entry and available audio.
